Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SAT Arena ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — username, email address, and a password stored only as a salted cryptographic hash (we never store plaintext passwords).
  • Progress data — answers, XP, streaks, levels, badges, test scores, and per-topic proficiency so your training history works across devices.
  • Session data — a secure HttpOnly cookie keeps you logged in. No advertising trackers are set by SAT Arena itself.
  • Optional Google sign-in — if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive the email and name your Google account shares with us, and store Google's identifier to link the login.
  • Optional AI key — if you paste your own Gemini API key in Settings, it is stored on our server to power your AI tutor. You can remove it at any time.
  • Payment data — payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we only record the outcome and plan.

2. AI tutor

Your tutor questions are sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini or Groq) only when a key is configured, and only to generate an answer. Without a key, tutoring happens on our server with a built-in engine and nothing leaves it. Please don't share personal or sensitive information in tutor chats.

3. Advertising

The free plan may show ads from third-party networks (e.g., Google AdSense). Those networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com. Premium members see no ads.

4. Kids and COPPA

SAT Arena is intended for students 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

5. Where data lives

Your data is stored in a hosted SQLite database (Turso) and on our hosting provider's servers. By using the Service you consent to this storage.

6. Your rights

  • Access and export — email us and we'll provide the account data we hold.
  • Deletion — email us to delete your account and associated data.
  • Correction — update your profile and settings in-app, or ask us.

7. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Deleted accounts have their data removed from the database.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted on this page. Continued use after changes means you accept them.

9. Contact
