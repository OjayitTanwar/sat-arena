Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Subscriptions (monthly and annual)

  • You can cancel at any time from the Premium page. Your premium access continues until the end of the period you already paid for, no partial refunds for the unused portion.
  • If you believe you were charged in error, or you didn't use premium at all, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we'll issue a full refund.

Lifetime purchases

  • Lifetime is a one-time purchase. If you bought it within the last 14 days and haven't meaningfully used premium, we'll refund it in full.
  • After 14 days, lifetime purchases are non-refundable except in cases of a fault with the Service that we cannot fix within a reasonable time.

How to request a refund

Email tanwarojayit@gmail.com with the email address on your account and the date of the charge. We aim to respond within 3 business days. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method through Stripe.

Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank, please contact us first, we'd rather sort it out directly. Accounts with repeated successful chargebacks may be closed.
